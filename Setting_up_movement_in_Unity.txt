Setting up movement in Unity:

Edit → Project Settings → Player → Other Settings → Active Input Handling → both
Make all platforms new layer called Ground, in player movement script put ground layer to ground (so that groundcheck works)
Create new asset → 2d → physics material 2d, set friction = 0 → drag into player’s box collider material
Create empty child of player called ground check, put at player’s feet, drag into groundcheck thing in player movement script (after saving script)

Walking Enemy:
Create 2 empty objects → drag left bound of movement into pointA and right bound of movement into pointB in the enemy walking script section (after saving script)
Play around with movespeed, moving pointA and pointB

Jumping Enemy:
Play around with gravity scale, jumping power, jumping interval
